Investment process

From raw market data to executed trades

The process is systematic end to end. Discretion is limited to research decisions and risk oversight, never to individual trades.

  1. 01

    Data

    Market, order-book and on-chain data, cleaned and checked before any research use.

  2. 02

    Signals

    Independent models, each validated out of sample before it receives capital.

  3. 03

    Portfolio

    Signals combined under a risk budget, so no single model or asset dominates.

  4. 04

    Risk

    Hard limits on exposure, concentration and drawdown, monitored around the clock.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Automated execution across liquid venues, measured against expected costs.

Don’t trust, verify. The crypto principle applies to our own models first. Nothing trades until the data supports it.
